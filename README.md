# Satellite

Satellite gathers the minimap buttons of your addons, and Blizzard's expansion button (order hall,
Omnium Folio...), into one launcher, so your minimap stays clear. Open the launcher to reach them
all in one place.

Satellite is in early development. Click the button with the coloured discs on your minimap's
ring to open its menu.

## Settings

**Beside the menu**: right-click the launcher. The menu stays open while you change things, so
you see them at once:

- The style: a button with coloured discs on the minimap's ring (drag it around the ring), the
  compass arrow, a circle or square, a side bar on any edge of the screen, or the menu always
  open. Drag the circle or square against an edge of the screen and it docks there, partly past
  the edge. Elsewhere, choose which way their menu opens: toward the middle of the screen, left,
  right, up or down.
- The launcher's size (50–200%), the button size, how many buttons go down each column of the
  menu, the addons' names in the menu, and for the compass arrow: in place of the minimap's west
  arrow or beside it.
- On the **Buttons** tab: untick buttons to take them out of the menu, drag them or use the
  arrows to change the order, or sort them by name again. Below the list: hide the addons' own
  minimap buttons (or keep them), show addons without a minimap button too (only in the addon
  compartment, or only for broker displays), and include Blizzard's expansion button.
- Locked or not: unlocked, drag the launcher to move it; locked, Shift-drag still moves it.
  "Reset position" puts it back.
- The border colour (gold, silver, bronze or a quiet version of your class colour) and the
  colours of the small symbols (coloured, silver, gold, bronze or class colour), and the
  symbols themselves: the default dragon, potion and sword, or one for your class (skulls for a
  Death Knight).

**In Blizzard's Options**, on the AddOns tab (or type `/satellite`): the same button options, and
a button that opens the launcher settings.

## Commands

| Command              | What it does           |
|----------------------|------------------------|
| `/satellite`         | Open the settings      |
| `/satellite list`    | List the buttons found |
| `/satellite reset`   | Put the launcher back  |
| `/satellite debug`   | Toggle debug output    |

## Languages

English, German, French, Italian, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese (simplified and
traditional) and Korean.
